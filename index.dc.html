Common questions

Do you only work on game assets, or only on video creatives?+

Both, by design. Most clients start with one and move to the other once they see the same team keeps the visual style consistent. Assets built for the game become the raw material for the ads that market it.

What file formats and engines do you deliver into?+

2D and 3D source files (.psd, .aep, .blend, .ma/.mb) plus engine-ready exports for Unity, Unreal, and Spine. If you already have a pipeline or brand guidelines, we adapt to it rather than asking you to adapt to us.

How fast can you turn around a video creative?+

Performance-focused cuts typically ship in days once source assets are ready; full cinematic trailers take longer depending on scene complexity. We scope timelines per project rather than quoting one number for everything.

Can you start before our game has any assets at all?+

Yes, we regularly join projects at the concept stage, when a game exists only as a design doc, and build the first characters, UI, and environment art from there.

What genres do you have the most experience in?+

Strategy, battlers, puzzle, RPG and casual titles make up most of our project history.

Do you offer ongoing support or only one-off projects?+

Both. Some clients bring a single campaign; most stay on a monthly retainer so the same team keeps producing assets and creatives as the game evolves.

How does pricing work?+

Every engagement starts with a fixed-price pilot (Hook Sprint $1,000, Playable Pilot $450, Style Match Test $400). Retainers are priced from a published floor and cancel monthly. Larger projects are quoted within 24 hours. See pricing →

How do you use AI?+

Game assets are AI-assisted and human-authored, with a per-asset log and a No-AI mode if you need it. UA creatives are AI-native and brand-locked to your source files. We never upload your materials to tools that train on customer data. Read the AI policy →
