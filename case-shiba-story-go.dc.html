Client: Proof of Play · Genre: roguelike RPG · Scope: art direction support, character & environment art, Spine animation, trailer, UA campaign creatives.

TASK

[THE BRIEF]

Proof of Play needed an art partner that could help define the visual style of Shiba Story Go!, ship a growing cast of enemies and bosses on a fixed weekly rhythm, and then turn the same assets into a trailer and a steady stream of UA creatives — without a second vendor and without the style drifting between game and ads.

WEEK

[HOW A WEEK LOOKED]

Monday

Kickoff call across time zones: this week's character list, priorities, references locked. Daily written updates start.

Tue – Wed

Sketches and silhouettes in the shared tracker. Art Director comments in-thread; revisions turned same day.

Thursday

Finals, Spine rigs and idle / attack / hit sets. Atlases exported for the engine.

Friday

Weekly drop with sources. Approved characters move to the creative team for the next UA batch.

LESSONS

[WHAT WE WOULD DO DIFFERENTLY]

  • Lock the style guide in writing in week one, not week three. Every later character would have cost one round less.
  • Start UA hooks the same week the first three characters were approved, instead of waiting for the trailer. Creatives die in nine days; the pipeline should start early.
  • Track creative test results next to the asset tracker from day one, so art decisions can be informed by what converts.
