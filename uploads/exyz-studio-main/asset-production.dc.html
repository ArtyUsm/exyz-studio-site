Game asset outsourcing without the style drift

EXYZ.Studio is a 2D game art studio producing concept art, character design, level backgrounds, and UI for mobile games. We work as an outsourced art department: from the first sketch to production-ready, engine-friendly files, keeping a single consistent art style across hundreds of assets.

Our Spine animation team rigs and animates every character we design — idle, attack, hit, and custom sequences — delivered as Spine projects with atlases ready for Unity, Unreal, or your custom pipeline. The animated characters on this page are live Spine previews, not videos.

Studios choose us for full-cycle asset production on titles like Shiba Story Go!, where we shipped 60+ characters, bosses, and levels on a weekly cadence. If you need reliable game art outsourcing that scales with your roadmap, start a project and we'll scope the first batch together.
